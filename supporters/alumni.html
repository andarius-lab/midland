Alumni

 

 

 

 

With over 300 Midland alumni to choose from, we're sure you're doing and achieving some amazing things. To help spread the word, we'll be featuring new alumni success stories whenever we get them. We're counting on you to let us know what's happening out there, so send us news if you have news of a former Midland Mustang!  

Alumni Facebook                       Alumni Twitter                      Alumni Instagram

 

 

Are you ready for an exciting alumni weekend? Mark your calendars now, because this one is going to be great!

This year the annual Alumni Weekend will be on October 11th, 2025. It’s a wonderful opportunity to reconnect with old friends, relive cherished memories, and see how Midland Adventist Academy continues to grow and thrive.

Don’t miss out!

Weekend Schedule

  • Sabbath Service 

    • Doors Open @ 10 AM​

    • Program Starts @ 10:30 AM

    • Speaker: Richard Paradise

  • Lunch @ 12 PM​

  • Alumni Games

    • Volleyball @ 7:30 PM​

    • Girls Basketball @ 8:30 PM

    • Boys Basketball @ 9:30 PM

You might be asking, “Who is alumni weekend for?”

If you have EVER attended Midland, as a student or staff member, even for only one year, YOU ARE AN ALUMNUS! And we can’t wait to see you!

This year we are recognizing the following classes:

  • Class of 2000 = 25 year

  • Class of 2005 = 20 year

  • Class of 2010 = 15 year

  • Class of 2015 = 10 year

  • Class of 2020 = 5 year

Whether you graduated last year or decades ago, this weekend is all about celebrating the spirit and community that makes Midland Adventist Academy special. Bring your families and share in the joy of reconnecting with old classmates and making new memories. Please spread the word and invite every former Midland family to celebrate with us!
