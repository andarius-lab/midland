Boys MS Basketball 2017-2018

The Middle School Mustangs began their 2017 season on November 14, playing Metro at home. The squad had seven additional games on schedule. Go Mustangs!

Season stats (updated after each game):

 Season record: 2-1
 Biggest victory margin: 60

 Fewest points scored: 22
 Closest game: 7

Coach Biographies

Tanner Finnegan, Head Coach:  Coach Tanner returns for a third year as head coach for the MS Boys basketball team this season. He is a Midland graduate (class of 2014), a thirteen-year senior, and played Mustang basketball since sixth grade (think he doesn't bleed Midland red?) Tanner is currently a senior at Avila University School of Business, and hopes to teach his middle school guys the necessary fundamentals to succeed as basketball players, while also being a good role model for life off the court.
